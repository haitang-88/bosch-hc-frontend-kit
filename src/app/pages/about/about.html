About Bosch HC Frontend Kit

An open-source Angular starter kit designed to accelerate frontend development in alignment with Bosch's design and engineering standards.

Our Mission

The Bosch HC Frontend Kit was created to provide developers with a solid, production-ready starting point for building Angular applications that follow Bosch's brand guidelines and best practices.

By standardising the project structure, design tokens, and component library, we help teams build consistent, high-quality interfaces faster.
