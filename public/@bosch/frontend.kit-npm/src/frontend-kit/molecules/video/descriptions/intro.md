The `video` element serves the purpose of embedding video content within documents.

It can be used with or without a caption.

The optional available settings are:

- playback rate
- download
- picture-in-picture

<div class="frontend-kit__notification a-notification -warning"><i class="a-icon ui-ic-alert-warning"></i><div class="a-notification__content">
Due to the same-origin policy, certain browsers may open the video download in a new tab instead of initiating an automatic download. Users must ensure that the download starts properly by checking the new tab for the file or by modifying the aforementioned policy.</div></div>

<div class="frontend-kit__notification a-notification -warning"><i class="a-icon ui-ic-alert-warning"></i><div class="a-notification__content">
To provide a seamless experience, users are encouraged to upload their own subtitle tracks. Unfortunately, due to browser restrictions, we are unable to display subtitles within the player. This is essential for deaf users to follow along. More informations can be found <a href="https://dequeuniversity.com/rules/axe/2.1/video-caption" target="_self">here</a>.</div></div>
